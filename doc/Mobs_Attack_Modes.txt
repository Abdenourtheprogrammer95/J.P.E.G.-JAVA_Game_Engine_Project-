Hostile mobs:
*** Zombies are the slowest mobs; they punch you with their fists.
*** Skeletons are range-attack mobs; they can shoot you with their bow from a set distance.
*** Phantoms (now deprecated) are mobs that will distance themselves from you, only to come back rushing at you with a speed boost.
* Creepers are kamikaze: they self-explode when at a set distance from the player, damaging anything in the area.

Passive mobs (by choice):
*** Endermen are fast travelers who can also teleport, but they don’t attack.
*** Spiders crawl towards you, but don’t hit you.
